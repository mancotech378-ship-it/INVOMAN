## 1. Introduction

Welcome to INVOMAN, a GST invoicing, billing, and business management application developed by MANCOTECH.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information INVOMAN may collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

By using INVOMAN, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, INVOMAN may process the following information:

### Business Information

- Business or company name
- Business address and contact details
- GSTIN and other tax-related information
- Business logo
- Bank and payment details entered for invoicing

### Customer and Supplier Information

- Customer and supplier names
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Billing and shipping addresses
- GSTIN, where applicable
- Customer account balances and transaction history

### Invoice and Financial Information

- Invoices and estimates
- Products and services
- Prices, discounts, and taxes
- Payment records and payment methods
- Customer advances and credit balances
- Outstanding amounts
- Customer account statements
- Business expenses and financial reports

### Technical Information

Depending on the application features and services enabled, technical information such as app version, device information, diagnostic logs, crash reports, or analytics events may be processed.

INVOMAN does not require users to provide sensitive personal information unrelated to its billing and business-management functions.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed through INVOMAN is used to:

- Create and manage GST invoices and estimates
- Maintain customer and supplier records
- Record payments, advances, and outstanding balances
- Generate invoices, receipts, and account statements in PDF format
- Calculate applicable taxes
- Manage products, services, and business expenses
- Support backup and restoration features
- Enable synchronization where available and activated
- Improve application reliability, security, and performance
- Provide technical support when requested

We do not use customer invoice information for unrelated marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Storage

INVOMAN is designed to maintain business records in an application database on the user's device.

Information stored locally remains on the device unless the user initiates an action that transfers it, such as exporting a document, sharing an invoice, creating a backup, or using an enabled synchronization service.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and maintaining appropriate backups of important business records.

If cloud storage or synchronization is offered, the applicable service and its data-handling practices should be disclosed to users before activation.

## 5. Data Sharing

INVOMAN may allow users to share invoices, estimates, receipts, and customer statements through email, messaging applications, or other services selected by the user.

When users choose to share a document, the receiving application or service may process that document under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information or customer financial records to data brokers.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application.

## 6. Payments and UPI

INVOMAN may generate payment QR codes or payment links using business payment details entered by the user.

Where supported, payment requests may open third-party UPI or banking applications.

Payments completed through those services are subject to the respective payment provider's terms and privacy practices.

INVOMAN's payment-recording features should not be interpreted as confirmation that a payment has been processed by a bank or payment network.

## 7. Permissions

INVOMAN may request permissions necessary for features such as:

- Accessing images or documents selected by the user for business logos
- Saving, opening, or sharing generated PDF files
- Accessing the internet for enabled online features
- Sending notifications where notification functionality is available

Permissions are requested according to the requirements of the relevant feature and Android version.

Users may manage applicable permissions through their device settings.

## 8. Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

INVOMAN may integrate third-party services for application functionality, diagnostics, analytics, advertising, or other enabled features.

Such providers may process technical identifiers or usage information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Where applicable, we will provide appropriate disclosures and consent options required by law or platform policies.

**Third-party services actually integrated into the published version of INVOMAN should be identified here before publication.**

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by INVOMAN.

These measures may include Android application isolation, controlled database access, and platform-supported security features.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate authentication and avoid sharing confidential business documents with unauthorized recipients.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records stored locally are generally retained until they are deleted through available application functions or the application's local data is removed.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored records if no backup exists.

Copies previously exported, shared, or backed up may remain in their respective destinations.

If cloud synchronization or server-side accounts are introduced, this policy will explain the applicable retention periods and account-data deletion procedures.

Users may contact us regarding privacy questions or requests involving personal information controlled by MANCOTECH.

## 11. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable laws and the features available in the application, users may:

- Review and update business or customer information
- Correct inaccurate records
- Export available business documents
- Delete supported records
- Manage application permissions
- Request information about personal data processed by MANCOTECH

Some accounting records may need to be retained where required by applicable tax or other legal obligations.

## 12. Children's Privacy

INVOMAN is intended for business and professional use and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information for independent use of the application.

If we become aware of personal information collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

## 13. International Data Processing

If third-party cloud, analytics, or other online services are enabled, information may be processed in locations outside the user's country.

Where applicable, such processing will be subject to relevant legal safeguards and service-provider arrangements.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, applicable law, or our data-handling practices.

The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
