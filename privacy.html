Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Developer: MANCOTECH · Application: INVOMAN

MANCOTECH respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use INVOMAN, a GST invoicing, billing, and business management application.

1. Information processed

Depending on features used, INVOMAN processes information you enter, including business names, contact details, addresses, GSTIN, business logos, customer and supplier details, product and service information, invoices, estimates, expenses, payment records, customer advances, credit balances, and account statements.

Where enabled, technical information such as device characteristics, app version, diagnostic information, usage events, and advertising identifiers may be processed by integrated services.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage invoices, calculate taxes, maintain business records, track payments and customer credits, generate PDFs and reports, support backup or synchronization features where available, and maintain app reliability and security.

3. Local storage and backups

Business records may be stored in the application's local database on your device. Information may leave the device when you choose to export, share, back up, or synchronize records, or when integrated online services operate. Backup and synchronization availability depends on your app version and configuration.

4. Sharing

When you share an invoice, statement, or other document, the receiving app or service handles that document under its own privacy practices. We do not sell customer invoice records to data brokers. We may disclose information if legally required.

5. Payments and UPI

INVOMAN may generate QR codes or links using payment information supplied by the business. Transactions initiated in external UPI or banking apps are subject to those providers' terms and privacy policies. Recording a payment in INVOMAN does not independently verify receipt of funds.

6. Device permissions

Depending on enabled features and Android version, INVOMAN may request access to user-selected files or images, document-saving and sharing capabilities, internet access, and notifications. You can manage applicable permissions in Android settings.

7. Third-party services

Some versions may integrate advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud services. These services may process technical identifiers and usage information under their own policies. Specific providers and data practices depend on the released app version and should be reflected in its Google Play Data safety declaration.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please protect your device and keep independent backups of important financial records.

9. Retention and deletion

Locally stored business data generally remains until removed using available app functions or device-level app data controls. Uninstalling or clearing app data can permanently remove records unless backed up. Exported or shared copies may remain with their recipients or destinations. If server-side account storage is enabled, applicable deletion procedures may differ; contact us for assistance.

10. Your choices

Subject to applicable law and app functionality, you may review, correct, export, or delete supported records, manage device permissions, and contact us with privacy questions. Legal recordkeeping obligations may limit deletion of some records.

11. Children

INVOMAN is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

12. International processing

If third-party online services are enabled, some data may be processed outside India, subject to applicable requirements.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the application or applicable requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
